# Content status conventions

- Draft: incomplete material not ready for learners.
- AI-assisted: automated assistance was used; this label does not imply review.
- Under review: awaiting technical or editorial review.
- Verified: checked against appropriate primary references by a reviewer.
- Published: available to learners; not automatically synonymous with independently verified.
- Outdated: may no longer reflect current platform behavior.
- Archived: retained for history but no longer recommended.

Do not promote content to Verified without performing and recording the review.

## Origin labels (added in 0.2.0)

- `original`: written by the project owner.
- `ai-assisted`: written with AI assistance. Shown as "AI-assisted · unreviewed" until a review is recorded.
- `imported`: derived from a third-party source; requires a recorded license and a human rewrite.

`published` describes availability. `verified` is only valid with a recorded reviewer and date; the validator rejects it otherwise.
